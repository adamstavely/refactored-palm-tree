Critical Minerals Supply Chain Assessment:
Rare Earth Processing Vulnerabilities, 2025–2028
IIR-2026-0441 Produced: 24 MAY 2026 Analyst: STAVELY_A State: DRAFT

This assessment examines structural vulnerabilities in allied rare earth supply chains with particular attention to processing concentration as the primary near-term risk. The assessment draws on all-source collection from Q4 2024 through May 2026.

Key judgment: Processing concentration, not export control policy, represents the most durable structural risk to allied rare earth supply chains through the 2025–2028 horizon. This assessment is supported by GRND sources with medium confidence.

Collection gaps exist against TECHINT and HUMINT disciplines. See collection gap annex (Appendix C). Two gaps have been submitted as collection requirements to TIS/DIKE.

Mitigation strategies assessed in Section 4 include IRA processing provisions, CRMA benchmarks, and near-term stockpile adequacy. Allied processing capacity investment timelines remain optimistic relative to historical project delivery rates.
